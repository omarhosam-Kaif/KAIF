Espresso or filter: which is right for you?

Espresso is a small, strong shot made under pressure, so the body feels heavy and the flavour is concentrated. Filter is a larger, lighter cup that shows more of the coffee's fruit and floral notes. Choose espresso if you drink milk drinks or want a strong shot. Choose filter if you want to taste the origin.
