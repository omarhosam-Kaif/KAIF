FAQ

Coffee questions, answered by the nerds who roast them.

Is my coffee fresh?

We roast in small batches, so every bag is made close to when it reaches you. Want to know the roast date of your bag? Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll tell you.

Should I buy whole beans or ground coffee?

Whole beans, always. Coffee loses its flavour fast once it’s ground, so grind it just before you brew. Every bag in our shop is whole beans.

What grind do I need?

Espresso: fine. Pour-over or filter: medium. French press: coarse. Too fine for your brewer tastes bitter, and too coarse tastes sour and weak.

How should I store my beans?

Keep them in the sealed bag or an airtight jar, somewhere cool and dark. Skip the fridge, since it adds moisture. If you won’t finish them within a month, freeze them in small portions.

How many cups does a 250g bag make?

Roughly 15 to 18 filter cups, or about 13 espresso shots, depending on how strong you like it.

What’s the difference between single origin, blends and flavoured?

Single origin tastes like one farm or region, so you get its character straight. Blends mix beans to balance the flavour. Flavoured bags have added flavour on top of Arabica and Robusta beans.

Is the decaf really decaf?

Our decaf is 99.9% decaffeinated, so you can enjoy a cup late at night without the buzz.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver in Cairo and Giza. We confirm the delivery fee and timing with you by phone or WhatsApp before we ship.

How do I order?

Add your bags to the cart and place the order. We confirm every order by phone or WhatsApp before we ship.

I can’t decide which bag to pick.

Tell us what you enjoy, whether fruity, chocolatey or nutty, and we’ll point you to the right bag. Message us on WhatsApp anytime.
